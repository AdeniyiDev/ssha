SECURE SIGHT - HOME PAGE SLIDER AND TILE PICTURES

Save these in THIS folder (images/hero/). .jpg is best; .webp, .png and .jpeg also work for the banners.
Each slide already has a built-in drawing (images/hero/art/*.svg). Your own banner picture, when you add it with the name below, covers the drawing.
Until then the drawing shows - nothing breaks.

SLIDER BANNERS (the picture fills the whole slide behind the text)
  Size: 1920 x 800 px (anything 1600 px wide or more, same shape, is fine). Under ~300 KB.
  Keep the LEFT 45% of the picture empty (plain background) because the text sits there.
  Put your products on the RIGHT side - except the curtain slide, which is the other way round.

  slide-1-locks.jpg           3D facial and biometric locks     (light background, locks on the right)
  slide-2-tuya-devices.jpg    Smart home Tuya devices           (dark background, devices on the right)
  slide-3-simplify.jpg        Simplify your life...             (light background, devices on the right)
  slide-4-curtains.jpg        Transform your space...           (white background, curtain on the LEFT, right side empty)

  app-logos.png               Strip of the app logos (Tuya, Smart Life, eWeLink, Alexa, Google Home), about 600 x 90 px,
                              transparent or white background. Shown on slide 3 under the text. Optional.

  slide-0-home.jpg            OPTIONAL full-slide banner for slide 1 (the "Build Your Smart Home" slide). If you add it,
                              it covers the text and drawing, so only use a finished banner that already has its own text.

TO CHANGE A SLIDE: open js/data.js and edit the HERO list (title, text, button link, colour).
TO CHANGE THE SPEED: HERO_DELAY in js/data.js (5000 = 5 seconds).
